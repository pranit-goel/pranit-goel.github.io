Cool Problems

A collection of some of my favourite problems:

  1. A rectangle can be covered by 100 circles of radius 2 (overlaps are allowed, but cutting is not). Prove that it can be covered by 400 unit circles.

  2. Opposite corners of an 8 × 8 chessboard are removed. Prove that the remaining board cannot be tiled by 1 × 2 dominoes.

  3. A wants to send a secret message inside a box to B through a postman. If A locks the box, B will not be able to open it. If A leaves it unlocked, the postman might see the secret. How can A send the message securely?
    Hint: The postman can make the journey multiple times.
